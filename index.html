How to play

1The basics

  • First to 10 points wins the match. Three touches max per side.
  • Jump first, then tap Space for a serve that clears the net. You have six seconds to serve.
  • The ball takes the colour of whoever touched it last.
  • Choose a map before playing. Graveyard has an impaling spike; Crematorium has a steam pipe that pushes players away; Dead Circus has a flexible stitched pole. Every round restores both players and resets the divider.

2Flips and dives

  • Tap Space while any attached body part touches the floor to dive toward the ball with outstretched arms.
  • Hold the same key in the air during a rally: the doll tucks, play briefly slows and a nearby ball is drawn in front of you.
  • Release to front-flip toward the net. The feet come over the top to kick the ball over, or strike the opponent or a hazard they meet.
  • More charge kicks harder and knocks back a nearby opponent.
  • A kick to the head stuns for three seconds: controls drop to 20% strength and charging slows. Stars show the stun.
  • Two flips at the net at once clash and redirect the ball.
  • Flips and dives share a 45-second cooldown that resets every round. Serving is always available.

3Hazards

Both gravestone buttons on the walls share one shuffled queue, previewed at the top of the screen. Aim for your opponent’s button.

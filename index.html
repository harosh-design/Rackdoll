Ragdoll Volleyball

One-on-one volleyball with hazards.

Mode
Left CPU
Opponent
Level 6, the champion, reads the ball's flight, aims its returns away from you and lines up its swing. Same rules for both players.
Hazards
Controls
Click a key to change it.
Tap your serve / hit key to serve. Hold during a rally, then release to hit. Use the other hand key to swing the other arm. The chosen arm winds up while play briefly slows, drawing a nearby ball toward that hand. Release for a powerful hit; more charge knocks back a nearby opponent or an executer your hand connects with. Release as your hand meets the ball for a perfect hit. Two simultaneous hits by the net clash and redirect the ball. After a hit, both hands share a 45-second cooldown that resets every round. Serving remains available. Jump first for a serve that clears the net. Three touches max, and six seconds to serve. Both yellow wall buttons share one shuffled queue of five hazards, previewed at the top of the screen: a slow magnet that drags you and the ball toward it, a boxer that punches any part of you, a fast comet that rams you away from the ball, a spring that slams harder the longer its run, and a slime that sticks to you and glues your limbs together. Jump to tear its glue, or swing the slimed arm to fling it off. Aim for your opponent’s button. Every third point spawns a gift, alternating sides. Reach it within 10 seconds for a random 30-second power: giant, tiny, feather ball (the ball falls slowly over your half), high jump, speed, magnet hands, or a shield against knockback. Bees fly in from the edges too, each showing its power: a big bee makes you giant, a tiny one tiny. Hit one with the ball you last touched and the power is yours.

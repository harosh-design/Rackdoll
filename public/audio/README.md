# Game sound effects

These 13 one-shots were generated locally with Stable Audio 3 Medium (MLX),
eight sampling steps, seeds 4101–4113. The prompts described isolated,
friendly arcade sports effects without music or voices. Each output was
trimmed to its useful sound, faded at the edges, peak-normalized, and encoded
as mono Ogg Vorbis. Total size: about 136 KB.

`bump`, `clack`, `thud`, and `serve` cover ball play. `perfect`, `counter`,
`rescue`, and `miss` cover special moves. `point` and `lose` signal scoring.
`button`, `rearm`, and `executer` cover hazards.
